Introducing my new [projects](/proj/) page!

I thought it would be fun to embed the `README` for some of my projects on my
website, so I used Hugo's
[`resources.GetRemote`](https://gohugo.io/functions/resources/getremote/) to
download it and
[`strings.replaceRE`](https://gohugo.io/functions/strings/replacere/) to
point relative links back to the repo (plus some other processing).

The part I'm excited about is the timestamp on the index page though. It is the
timestamp of the latest commit for that project, so it's a quick way to see the
recent projects I'm working on. It's also a good motivator for me to give an
old project some love.
